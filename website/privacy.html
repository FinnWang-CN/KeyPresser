Privacy policy

This policy explains how KeyPresser handles information on its website and in the online account and membership service for KeyPresser Professional.

Information we use

  • Accounts and devices. We use your email address, verification and sign-in information, device identifiers and names, and sign-in or device activity times to secure accounts, manage authorized devices, and activate licenses.
  • Membership and orders. We use order references, payment status, amounts, and membership dates to activate access, keep transaction records, and resolve billing or licensing issues.
  • Support. We use the contact information and details you provide to investigate your request and reply.
  • Website and browser storage. The website uses Baidu Analytics to measure visits. Your browser can store your language preference. The membership portal uses browser storage to remember your device and keep you signed in.

Checkout and service providers

For domestic orders, checkout uses the Xunhu Pay gateway and payment is processed by Alipay. For international orders where PayPal Checkout is available, PayPal processes the information you enter under its Privacy Statement. KeyPresser sends order details, such as the order reference and amount, and receives payment status to activate membership and assist with orders.

Hosting and email services help us operate the website, run the membership service, and send account verification emails. Links to external sites are subject to those sites’ own privacy notices.

Retention and your choices

Account, device, and order records are used while needed to provide the service and to handle security, support, disputes, or recordkeeping obligations. Where applicable, you can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information.

For privacy requests, use the private messaging option described under KeyPresser support. Keep your account details out of public issue reports.
