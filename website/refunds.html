KeyPresser Pricing

Refund policy

This policy applies to KeyPresser Professional membership purchases made using WeChat Pay. The 12-month membership is a one-time purchase and has no automatic renewal.

How to request a refund

Contact KeyPresser support privately with your account email, order reference, payment date, and reason for the request. Your order reference is available under My Orders in the Membership Center.

Eligibility and withdrawal rights

KeyPresser reviews payment errors, duplicate charges, activation issues, and other refund requests based on the order details and applicable law. Mandatory consumer and withdrawal rights continue to apply.

Activation problems

If payment succeeded but your membership did not activate, contact KeyPresser support so we can check the order. Please keep your order details and personal information out of public posts.
