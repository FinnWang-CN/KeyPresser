KeyPresser Pricing

Refund policy

This policy applies to KeyPresser Professional memberships purchased through Paddle Checkout. The 12-month membership is a one-time purchase and has no automatic renewal.

How to request a refund

Open the transaction link in your Paddle receipt or visit Paddle Buyer Support. Use the email address from your order and provide the transaction reference if requested. Paddle reviews requests and issues approved refunds.

Eligibility and withdrawal rights

Paddle's Refund Policy and Buyer Terms explain refund eligibility and any applicable withdrawal rights. Paddle may consider discretionary requests made within 14 days of the transaction. Mandatory consumer rights continue to apply.

Activation problems

If payment succeeded but your membership did not activate, contact KeyPresser support so we can check the order. Please keep your order details and personal information out of public posts.
