Which approach fits your task?

AutoHotkey is a Windows automation scripting language. It is a strong choice for custom hotkeys, programmable logic and workflows you want to maintain as scripts.

Source: Product website. This is a qualitative comparison, not a performance benchmark or compatibility test.

Consider KeyPresser Professional for basic graphical recording and playback when you prefer configuring a target window and repeat settings instead of writing a script. For complex logic, a script-based solution may be more appropriate.

Try a real workflow before paying

Use the Notepad++ recording tutorial to test a two-tab workflow. Verify the selected window and input mode, then test one replay before repeating. Background support varies by application; minimized windows may behave differently.
